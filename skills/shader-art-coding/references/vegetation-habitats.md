# Vegetation habitats

The [woodland edge shader](../templates/woodland-edge.glsl) draws an overhead vegetation study. Start with the fixed camera so changes in placement are easy to see. The stream animates while plants stay rooted.

**Follow a grove from water to meadow.** Each world-space cell supplies a stable founder and two smaller candidates nearby. Moisture favors alder on the banks. A slower site field admits oak groves on dry ground. Water excludes roots. The crown radius and position of each surviving tree contribute to canopy cover at the ground plant's root. Ground plants use that cover and the same moisture field to select damp shade for ferns, partial cover for shrubs, and open dry ground for flowers. These are illustrative habitat preferences. This small, stateless model generates one cohort of neighbors; it does not simulate years of competition or growth.

**Open a clearing.** Increase Clearing radius and compare the same patch in the two diagnostic views. In Root cover / moisture / crown silhouette, the red and blue channels recede while the green moisture field stays fixed. In Fern / edge shrub / meadow flowers, inspect the suitability channels even where tree crowns hide plants in the beauty view. Partially covered ground favors shrubs. A clearing beside a stream can remain too wet for meadow flowers.

**Widen the wet bank.** Restore the clearing, then increase Wet bank width. This changes species and understory suitability while preserving the stream path and existing candidate coordinates. In a mesh renderer, store the accepted placements with stable IDs and derive the canopy field from their actual crown footprints. Expand culling bounds to include swaying branches over a cell edge.

For a persistent landscape, age the stand offline. Give species different establishment delays, shade tolerances, dispersal radii and lifespans. Save the resulting stand and regenerate its understory after disturbances. SmartImposter implements generation-based stand growth and canopy-dependent understory. KingdomComeGlueMapper provides a separate example of preserving instance transforms when regrouping vegetation into sectors.
